Hyelin Nam

I'm a PhD Student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan, where I'm advised by Prof. JJ Park. Before that, I completed my master's at KAIST, where I was advised by Prof. Jong Chul Ye.

I am interested in video generative models and world models, exploiting their controllability to better capture physically plausible dynamics in generated videos.

I am open to research internships for Summer 2027 and collaborations. Feel free to reach out!
